Our Story

Welcome to Zees Delicious Crumbs Bakery!Zee's Delicious Crumbs Bakery started in 2024 in Nongoma, KwaZulu Natal. What began as ahome-based passion for baking has grown into a modest bakery dedicated to serving our community. a family-owned business with a passion for creating delicious, high-quality baked goods. Our journey began in 2024 when we started selling our fresh pastries at the local market.

Our Values

At Zees Delicious Crumbs Bakery, we are committed to providing exceptional customer service and using only the finest ingredients in our products. We believe in building lasting relationships with our customers and the community.

Meet Our Team

Our team is dedicated to bringing joy and flavor to every bite. From our skilled bakers to our friendly staff, we are all passionate about what we do.

Community Involvement

We are proud members of the Nongoma community and actively participate in local events and initiatives. Giving back is an essential part of who we are as a business.

Quality Assurance

We take pride in the quality of our products and the standards we maintain. Every item that leaves our bakery is carefully crafted to ensure it meets our high standards.

Customer Satisfaction

Your satisfaction is our top priority. We strive to exceed your expectations with every interaction and every product you receive from us.
